About

Photographer — Buenos Aires & London

I'm Francisco Murray, a photographer working between Buenos Aires and London, interested in ordinary light and the small rituals of city life.

I began photographing in black and white on a borrowed film camera and have spent most of the years since in the darkroom. The work is patient and observational — made by returning to the same streets, windows and people until they become unfamiliar again.

My current project, a first sustained body of work in colour, is ongoing. I'm available for editorial and portrait commissions, and selected prints are available on request.
